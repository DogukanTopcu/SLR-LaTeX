\section{Related Works}

Prior review literature on SLMs and SLM--LLM collaboration includes four closely related works. The closest is a systematic literature review (SLR) guided by the Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) 2020 statement, with a reproducible multi-database protocol. The study adopts a broad scope on SLMs and offers limited coverage of the 2025--2026 wave of hybrid and agent-based systems~\cite{slmreview2025prisma}. Three narrative surveys cover SLM definitions, training techniques, collaboration patterns, trustworthiness, and edge deployment. They do not follow a formal literature review framework~\cite{wang2024slmsurvey,chen2025collabmech,wang2025collabperf}. While existing surveys comprehensively detail SLM training techniques and model-level reasoning capabilities, they largely treat collaboration as a conceptual capability. Our study addresses this gap through a Kitchenham-guided synthesis that maps the actual architectural infrastructure---specifically, how routing mechanisms, edge--cloud bandwidth, and task decomposition directly affect operational constraints such as latency, API cost, and energy consumption.
