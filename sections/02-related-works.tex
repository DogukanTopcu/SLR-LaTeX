\section{Related Works}

Prior review literature on SLMs and SLM--LLM collaboration includes four closely related works. The closest is a PRISMA 2020-guided SLR with a reproducible multi-database protocol, but it adopts a broader scope on SLMs and offers limited coverage of the 2025--2026 wave of hybrid and agent-based systems~\cite{slmreview2025prisma}. Three narrative surveys cover SLM definitions, training techniques, collaboration patterns, trustworthiness, and edge deployment, but they do not provide a formal PRISMA-guided selection and extraction framework~\cite{wang2024slmsurvey,chen2025collabmech,wang2025collabperf}. Our study addresses this gap by providing a PRISMA-guided, engineering-oriented synthesis that aligns empirical evidence across domains and emphasizes operational metrics such as accuracy, latency, cost, and energy.
